Benefits are available to all full-time employees starting on your first day of employment. Part-time and contract workers should check with HR directly to understand what applies to their situation. Everything below — from medical coverage to your home office stipend — is accessible through the HR portal.

## Health insurance

We offer three medical plan options through your insurance carrier. All plans include access to the same provider network; the differences come down to premiums, deductibles, and how you want to manage out-of-pocket costs.

| Plan | Premium (employee) | Premium (employee + family) | Deductible |
|------|-------------------|----------------------------|------------|
| HSA-compatible HDHP | See HR portal for current rates | See HR portal for current rates | $1,500 |
| PPO Standard | See HR portal for current rates | See HR portal for current rates | $500 |
| PPO Premium | See HR portal for current rates | See HR portal for current rates | $250 |

<Tip>
  You must enroll or make changes within **30 days of your start date**. Outside of that window, your next opportunity is the annual open enrollment period, which typically runs in November. Don't miss it — changes cannot be made outside these windows without a qualifying life event.
</Tip>

## Dental and vision

Both dental and vision coverage are available at no cost for employee-only enrollment.

- **Dental** — $0/month for employee coverage. Covers two cleanings and X-rays per year at 100%.
- **Vision** — $0/month for employee coverage. Annual eye exam covered in full, plus an allowance for glasses or contacts (see HR portal for current allowance amounts).

## 401(k)

We match 4% of your salary up to the IRS annual limit, helping you build retirement savings from day one. Enroll through the HR portal; changes take effect the following pay period.

<Info>
  Vesting on matched contributions is **immediate** — there is no waiting period before the company match belongs to you.
</Info>

## Other perks

- **Home office stipend** — One-time allowance for new hires to set up your workspace. See the [home office setup](/policies/remote-work/home-office-setup) page and the HR portal for the current amount.
- **Learning & development** — Annual budget for courses, books, or conferences. Submit reimbursements through the HR portal (see HR portal for current annual amount).
- **Wellness** — Monthly reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness apps, or mental health services (see HR portal for current reimbursement amount).
- **Parental leave** — See the HR portal for current parental leave entitlements for primary and secondary caregivers.

## Enrollment and changes

Log in to the HR portal to enroll, view plan documents, or make changes during a qualifying life event such as marriage, the birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.

Questions? Reach out to the People team in `#people-help` on Slack.
