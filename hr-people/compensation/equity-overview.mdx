Equity is a meaningful part of total compensation for most roles at Mortega. It aligns your long-term interests with the company's growth and gives you real ownership in what you're building. This page explains the fundamentals — for the details specific to your grant, review your grant agreement or contact HR.

## Grant types

We issue equity in two forms depending on company stage and employee level.

**Stock options (ISOs / NSOs)** — The right to purchase shares at the strike price set on your grant date. Options have value when the company's fair market value rises above your strike price. ISOs (Incentive Stock Options) and NSOs (Non-Qualified Stock Options) have different tax treatments — your grant agreement will specify which type you received.

**RSUs (Restricted Stock Units)** — A promise of shares delivered upon vesting. RSUs have value as long as the company has any value at all, making them lower risk than options. They are common for later-stage companies and more senior roles.

Your offer letter specifies which type of equity you received.

## Vesting schedule

Standard vesting is **4 years with a 1-year cliff**:

- No shares vest until your 1-year anniversary with the company
- 25% of your total grant vests on that cliff date
- The remaining 75% vests monthly over the following 36 months

Refresher grants issued to tenured employees vest on the same monthly schedule, without a cliff.

## Exercising options

If you hold stock options, you must exercise them to convert to actual shares. You have a window during which you can do this:

- **While employed** — You can exercise any time after your options have vested.
- **After leaving** — You typically have 90 days from your last day to exercise vested options.

<Warning>
  The **90-day post-termination exercise window** is critical. If you leave the company and do not exercise your vested options within 90 days of your last day, those options expire and are forfeited. Check your grant agreement for the exact window that applies to you, as terms can vary.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The tax implications of exercising options differ significantly between ISOs and NSOs, and can be material depending on the spread between your strike price and the current fair market value. **Talk to a qualified tax advisor before exercising** — especially for a large grant or before a known liquidity event.
</Tip>

## Equity events

At a liquidity event — such as an IPO or acquisition — your vested shares become tradeable or are converted to cash. Unvested shares are typically subject to acceleration clauses in acquisition scenarios, which may cause some or all of them to vest early. Review your grant agreement for the specific terms that apply to you.
