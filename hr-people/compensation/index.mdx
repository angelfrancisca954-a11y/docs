Our compensation philosophy is to pay competitively within our market and to be transparent about how pay decisions are made. We want you to understand where your pay sits, why it's set where it is, and what influences it over time.

This section covers salary bands and equity. For information about how compensation changes through merit increases and promotions, see [performance reviews](/hr-people/performance-reviews).
