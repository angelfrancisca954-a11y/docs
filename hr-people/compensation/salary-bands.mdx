We use salary bands tied to role levels to make compensation decisions consistent and defensible across the company. Bands are reviewed annually against external market data to ensure we remain competitive. Understanding how they work gives you clarity on where your pay sits and what drives changes over time.

## Leveling

Each role has a level, typically L1 through L6 (or equivalent titles like Associate, Mid, Senior, Staff, and Principal). Levels reflect your scope of impact, the autonomy you're expected to exercise, and the contributions expected at that stage of your career.

Your offer letter includes your level. If you're unsure of your current level, ask your manager.

## Bands

Every level has a minimum, midpoint, and maximum. We target offers at or near the midpoint for candidates who are a strong fit for the role and level. The table below shows engineering titles as examples — equivalent structures apply across all functions.

## Pay adjustments

Compensation adjustments happen through three mechanisms:

1. **Annual review cycle** — Merit increases are tied to performance ratings and typically fall in the 0–8% range of base salary.
2. **Promotions** — Moving to a higher level generally brings your pay to the midpoint of the new band, reflecting the increased scope and expectations.
3. **Market corrections** — If our annual band analysis shows a role is below market, we may adjust compensation outside the normal review cycle.

## Geographic adjustments

We pay based on the market for the role, adjusted for cost of labor in your location. Remote employees in different regions may have different band ranges that reflect their local labor market. Contact HR for the specific band that applies to your location and role.
