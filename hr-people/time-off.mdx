We have an unlimited PTO policy for salaried employees. Unlimited means we trust you to take the time you need — it doesn't mean time off is boundless in practice. Most employees take three to five weeks per year, plus company holidays. Taking meaningful time away makes you more effective, and your manager expects you to use it.

## Types of leave

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vacation / personal time">
    There is no fixed accrual for vacation or personal days. You take time off by following the request process below. There is no balance to track and nothing carries over at year-end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sick leave">
    Use sick days when you're ill or need to care for a sick family member. No approval is needed — just notify your manager and mark it in the HR portal. For sick days, submit in the portal on the same day or the next business day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Company holidays">
    We observe approximately 10 federal holidays per year. The full calendar for the year is posted in the HR portal each January. These days are in addition to your personal PTO.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Parental leave">
    See the [benefits page](/hr-people/benefits) for full paid parental leave details, including the number of weeks available for primary and secondary caregivers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bereavement">
    You receive up to 5 days paid bereavement leave with no approval needed. If your situation requires more time, contact HR directly to discuss your options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Jury duty / civic leave">
    Jury duty and other civic leave are fully paid. Send your documentation to HR when you receive the summons.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Requesting time off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check with your manager first">
    Before submitting anything formally, check in with your manager — especially if the time overlaps with a launch window, on-call rotation, or other time-sensitive commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your request in the HR portal">
    For absences longer than two days, submit your request at least **two weeks in advance**. For sick days, submit on the day of your absence or the next business day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block your calendar and set an out-of-office reply">
    Once approved, block your calendar so teammates can see you're unavailable, and configure your out-of-office reply with a contact person for urgent matters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Blackout periods

Some teams have blackout periods around major product releases or critical customer commitments. Your manager will communicate these dates in advance. Requests submitted during a blackout period require both manager and director approval.

<Note>
  Blackout periods are the exception, not the rule. If your team has one coming up, your manager will let you know well ahead of time.
</Note>

## Carryover

Because PTO is unlimited, there is no balance to carry over at year-end and no PTO payout at termination.
