All access to Mortega systems follows the principle of least privilege: you get access to what you need to do your job, and nothing more. This keeps our systems secure and reduces risk if any account is ever compromised. Use the process below whenever you need access to a new tool, environment, or dataset.

## How to request access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a ticket">
    Open a request in the IT portal, or post in Slack at `#it-requests`. Either channel works for standard requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the required details">
    Your request must specify: the system or resource you need, the level of access required, and a clear business justification explaining why you need it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await manager approval">
    Your manager will receive an approval notification automatically. Most requests require manager sign-off; some require director or security team approval depending on the access level (see the table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your access">
    Approved requests are fulfilled within **one business day** for standard tools, and within **three business days** for production or customer data systems.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Access levels

The table below shows the four access tiers, what each one covers, and who must approve it.

| Level | Examples | Approver |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Standard** | Slack channels, Notion, Figma | Manager |
| **Elevated** | AWS non-prod, staging environments | Manager + IT |
| **Privileged** | Production systems, customer data | Manager + Security |
| **Admin** | Identity provider, billing systems | Director + Security |

## Shared credentials and service accounts

<Warning>
  Do not share your personal credentials with anyone, including teammates or managers. Shared personal credentials make audits impossible and compromise the security of your account and the systems it touches.
</Warning>

If a workflow genuinely requires a shared credential or service account, request one through IT. Service accounts are audited quarterly to ensure they remain necessary and are not over-permissioned.

## Access reviews

IT runs quarterly access reviews across all employees. You will receive an email listing your current active access — review it carefully and flag anything that is no longer needed for your role. Unused access is revoked automatically after 30 days of inactivity.

<Tip>
  Use the quarterly review as a prompt to clean up access from previous projects or roles. Reducing your own footprint is a simple way to improve Mortega's overall security posture.
</Tip>

## Offboarding

When an employee leaves Mortega, all access is revoked on their last day. If you are a manager with a departing team member, notify IT **at least two business days in advance** so that handoffs can be arranged before access is removed.

## Emergencies

For urgent access issues that are blocking critical work, contact the IT help desk directly on Slack at `#it-help` or by phone. Include your manager in the message so the request can be escalated quickly if needed.
