All devices used to access Mortega systems — whether company-owned or personal (BYOD) — must meet our minimum security requirements. These standards protect company data and ensure that a lost or compromised device doesn't become a larger incident. Review the requirements for your device type below.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Company-Issued Devices">
    Company laptops and phones are enrolled in MDM (Mobile Device Management) automatically when you receive them. MDM enforces the following controls on every company-issued device:

    - **Full-disk encryption** — all data on the device is encrypted at rest
    - **Screen lock after 5 minutes** of inactivity
    - **Approved OS versions** — your device will prompt you to update when a new approved version is available
    - **Endpoint security agent** — monitors for threats and policy violations
    - **Remote wipe capability** — allows IT to erase the device if it is lost or stolen

    <Warning>
      Do not attempt to remove MDM profiles or disable any security software on a company-issued device. Doing so may result in immediate loss of access to company systems and disciplinary action.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Personal Devices (BYOD)">
    If you use a personal device to access work email, Slack, or any other company system, you must meet the following minimum requirements before doing so:

    1. Enable a screen lock (PIN, password, or biometric)
    2. Enable full-disk encryption
    3. Keep the operating system up to date
    4. Avoid storing sensitive company data locally on the device

    Personal devices are **not** enrolled in MDM unless you voluntarily opt in. If IT needs to take action on a personal device during a security incident, only the work profile (where applicable) will be targeted — your personal data will not be touched.

    <Note>
      If you are unsure whether your personal device meets these requirements, reach out to IT via `#it-help` on Slack. They can walk you through checking your settings without enrolling your device in MDM.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Approved and prohibited uses

**Approved uses:**
- Work tasks, communication, and collaboration using company-approved tools
- Personal use that does not interfere with device security or performance

**Prohibited uses:**
- Installing software that circumvents or disables security controls
- Storing company data in personal cloud storage (use approved tools such as Google Drive or Notion)
- Connecting a company laptop to an untrusted network without first enabling VPN
- Sharing your device with family members for work-related tasks

## Lost or stolen devices

<Warning>
  Report a lost or stolen device to IT **immediately** — do not wait to see if it turns up. Every hour of delay increases the risk of unauthorized access to company systems and data.

  Contact IT via `#it-help` on Slack or by email. IT will remotely lock the device right away and initiate a remote wipe if necessary.
</Warning>

## End of employment

Return all company-issued devices on or before your last day at Mortega. Devices that are retained or not returned are subject to remote wipe, recovery action, and potential legal consequences.
