This section covers everything you need to know about working securely at Mortega — from requesting access to company systems and keeping your devices compliant, to recognizing phishing attempts and handling sensitive data responsibly. Every employee is expected to follow these policies from day one.

For urgent IT issues, contact the help desk directly on Slack at `#it-help`. For security incidents, follow the escalation path described in [Security Training](/it-security/security-training).
