Security training is required for every Mortega employee. You must complete your initial training within your first 30 days, and you will be notified when annual refresher training is due. Taking these modules seriously is one of the most effective things you can do to protect yourself and the company.

This section focuses on the two areas that account for the vast majority of real-world security incidents: recognizing and responding to phishing attempts, and handling company and customer data responsibly.
