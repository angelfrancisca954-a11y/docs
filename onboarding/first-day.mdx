Your first day is all about getting oriented — not delivering results. Follow this checklist in order to make sure your access, tools, and relationships are in place before anything else.

## Before You Start

Your manager sent a welcome email containing everything you need to kick things off:

- A link to activate your company email
- Your start date and first-day schedule
- A Slack invite (if your email is already active)

<Warning>
  If any of these items are missing from your inbox, contact your manager or HR before attempting any other tasks. You won't be able to complete the checklist without them.
</Warning>

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate your company email">
    Use the activation link in your welcome email. Set a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) immediately — do not skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your laptop">
    Follow the [laptop setup guide](/onboarding/tools/laptop-setup). IT ships all machines with a base image, but you'll still need to install any role-specific tools your team requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to VPN">
    VPN is required before you can access internal systems. Follow the [VPN setup guide](/onboarding/tools/vpn-setup) to connect for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your accounts">
    Log in to Slack, your project tracker, and the HR portal. Credentials are in your welcome email or in IT's onboarding ticket. See the full list of expected accounts in the [account access guide](/onboarding/tools/account-access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join your team channels">
    Ask your manager which Slack channels are relevant for your role. At minimum, join your team channel, `#ask-anything`, and `#announcements`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read today's standup notes">
    Before your first meeting, skim your team's most recent standup notes to get a quick sense of what everyone is working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet your onboarding buddy">
    HR pairs every new hire with an onboarding buddy. Reach out over Slack and set up a 30-minute intro call — they're your go-to for day-to-day questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you finish the checklist before the end of the day, use the extra time to read your team's most recent project brief or product spec. Context gathered early pays off fast.
</Tip>

## People to Meet Today

Make sure you connect — even briefly — with each of these people on your first day.

| Person | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Your manager | Walk through your first 30 days and set expectations |
| Your onboarding buddy | Day-to-day questions and cultural context |
| IT help desk | Confirm your laptop and account access setup |

<Note>
  You don't need deep conversations with everyone on day one. A quick Slack message or five-minute intro is enough to open the relationship.
</Note>
