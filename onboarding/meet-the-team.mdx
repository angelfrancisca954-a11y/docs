Understanding the shape of the organization is one of the fastest ways to unblock yourself. This page gives you a map of who works here, how the teams are structured, and where to go when you have a question or need a decision made.

## Leadership

The table below shows the senior leadership team and the best way to reach each person.

| Name | Role | Best contact |
|------|------|--------------|
| — | CEO | Slack DM for urgent items |
| — | CTO | Engineering escalations |
| — | VP People | HR and benefits questions |
| — | VP Engineering | Engineering org decisions |

<Note>
  Names are intentionally omitted from this published page. Your manager will introduce you to leadership during your first week, and the full employee directory in the HR portal has current details for everyone.
</Note>

## Teams

Each team below owns a distinct part of how Mortega builds and operates. Click into a card to learn more about how each team works.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Engineering" icon="code">
    Owns product infrastructure, services, and developer tooling. Organized into squads by domain. The primary owners of technical architecture, reliability, and the developer experience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product" icon="lightbulb">
    Works with Engineering and Design to define and ship features. Your primary contacts for roadmap context and prioritization decisions. Every squad has a dedicated PM.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design" icon="paintbrush">
    Handles product design, brand, and content design. Designers are embedded in product squads. For design reviews, ping the relevant squad designer directly in Slack.
  </Card>
  <Card title="People & Operations" icon="building">
    Covers HR, recruiting, IT, finance, and office operations. Come here for benefits questions, equipment requests, expense reimbursements, and anything related to your employment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Finding Anyone

The employee directory in the HR portal is the source of truth for the full company. Each entry includes role, team, location, and Slack handle.

<Tip>
  Not sure who owns something? Post in `#ask-anything` on Slack. Someone will either know the answer or point you to the right person — it's the fastest way to get unblocked when you're new.
</Tip>
