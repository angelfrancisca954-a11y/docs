We hold ourselves to a simple standard: treat everyone with respect, act with integrity, and take responsibility for your actions. These expectations apply everywhere you represent Mortega — in Slack, in code reviews, in meetings, and in every interaction with customers and colleagues.

## Our Expectations

**Respectful communication** — Disagree with ideas, not people. Criticism is welcome and necessary; personal attacks are not. This applies in Slack, in code reviews, in meetings, and everywhere else we interact.

**Inclusion** — We don't tolerate discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, national origin, or any other protected characteristic. We also don't tolerate exclusionary jokes or "banter" that makes colleagues feel unwelcome.

**Integrity** — Be honest with colleagues, managers, and customers. If you make a mistake, own it and fix it. Don't cover up errors or shift blame.

**Confidentiality** — Respect the confidentiality of company information and customer data. Don't share internal information with people who don't have a need to know.

**Conflicts of interest** — Disclose any situation where your personal interests could conflict with the company's. When in doubt, ask HR.

## What Counts as Harassment

Harassment includes (but is not limited to):

- Unwelcome comments about someone's appearance, identity, or personal life
- Persistent unwanted contact after someone has made clear they want to stop
- Sharing or posting someone's private information without consent
- Creating or forwarding material that demeans colleagues

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, you have several paths available to you. You don't need to handle it alone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Address it directly (if comfortable)">
    If you feel safe doing so, tell the person directly that their behavior is unwelcome. Sometimes people are unaware of the impact of their actions, and a direct conversation can resolve the issue quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report to your manager or HR">
    If a direct conversation isn't appropriate or didn't resolve the issue, report the behavior to your manager or HR. Provide as much detail as you can — what happened, when, and who was involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use anonymous reporting if needed">
    If the issue involves your manager, or you're not comfortable reporting openly, go directly to HR or use the anonymous reporting hotline. The link is available in the HR portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this policy. All reports are treated seriously and handled with discretion.
</Note>

## Consequences

Violations may result in a warning, performance improvement plan, or termination, depending on the severity of the behavior and whether it is part of a pattern. HR and leadership review each situation individually.
