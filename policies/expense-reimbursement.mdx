We reimburse reasonable, work-related expenses. "Reasonable" means what a thoughtful person would spend of their own money on a business trip or work task. When in doubt about whether something qualifies, ask your manager before spending — it's always easier to get approval upfront than to dispute a declined claim later.

## What's Covered

| Category | Covered | Not Covered |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Travel (business trips) | Flights, trains, approved hotels | First-class upgrades (unless pre-approved), personal side trips |
| Meals (travel days) | See the HR portal for daily meal limits | Alcohol (unless a team event) |
| Home office | See [home office setup](/policies/remote-work/home-office-setup) | Furniture beyond the stipend |
| Software and tools | Role-specific tools with manager approval | Subscriptions that duplicate company-provided tools |
| Team events | Team lunches, offsites with manager approval | Events without prior budget approval |
| Learning & development | Courses, books, conferences within the annual budget | Items outside the L&D budget cap |

## How to Submit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the expense tool">
    Access the expense tool via the link in the HR portal. Sign in with your company email credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new report and attach receipts">
    Create a new expense report and attach receipts for every line item. Receipts are required — expenses without documentation may be declined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Categorize and justify each item">
    Select the appropriate expense category for each line item and add a brief business justification. Be specific — "client dinner with Acme team" is better than "dinner."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for manager approval">
    Submit the completed report for your manager's review. You'll receive an email notification when it's been approved or if more information is needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Submit all expenses within **30 days** of the expense date. Late submissions may be declined regardless of whether the expense was otherwise reimbursable. Don't let receipts pile up.
</Warning>

## Approval and Reimbursement

Once you submit your report, here's what happens next:

- Your manager reviews and approves the report.
- Finance reviews and approves within 3 business days.
- Reimbursement is paid via payroll on the next pay cycle, or via direct ACH within 5–7 business days (depending on your setup).

## Corporate Card

Some roles have access to a corporate card. Corporate card expenses still require receipts and business justifications entered in the expense tool — they are not auto-approved. The same categorization and timeliness rules apply.

## Travel Bookings

Use the approved travel booking tool for all flights and hotels. Booking outside the tool requires prior approval and may not be reimbursed at full cost. See the [travel policy](/policies/travel-policy) for full booking guidelines.
