We want every remote employee to have a workspace that lets them do their best work. That means giving you both the tools and the financial support to set up a comfortable, productive environment from day one. Here's what's available and how to access it.

## New Hire Stipend

Full-time remote employees receive a **one-time home office stipend** to spend on equipment and furniture — see the HR portal for current stipend amounts. Submit receipts through the expense tool — see [expense reimbursement](/policies/expense-reimbursement) for the full submission process.

<Tip>
  You have **90 days from your start date** to submit stipend receipts. Don't wait until the last minute — set a reminder in your calendar now so you don't lose access to funds you're entitled to.
</Tip>

### What's Covered

- Monitor, keyboard, mouse, or other peripherals
- Desk, chair, or other ergonomic furniture
- Lighting or webcam for video calls
- Headset or headphones

### What's Not Covered

- General household items (lamps, shelves) that aren't primarily for work use
- Upgrades to items already provided by the company

## Company-Provided Equipment

IT ships your laptop before your start date. If your role requires additional equipment — such as an external monitor, docking station, or specific peripherals — request it through an [IT ticket](/it-security/access-requests). Your manager will need to approve the request.

## Internet Reimbursement

We reimburse a monthly allowance for home internet — see the HR portal for current reimbursement amounts. Submit monthly via the expense tool using the "Internet reimbursement" category. No pre-approval is needed for this reimbursement.

## Ergonomics

If you're experiencing discomfort from your current setup, contact HR. We can arrange an ergonomic assessment and cover additional equipment as needed. Your long-term health matters — don't push through pain.

## Co-Working Spaces

If you prefer not to work from home full-time, we'll reimburse a monthly allowance for a co-working space membership — see the HR portal for current reimbursement amounts. Submit through the expense tool with your manager's approval.

<Note>
  Co-working space reimbursements require your manager's approval each month. Loop them in before signing up for a membership so there are no surprises.
</Note>
