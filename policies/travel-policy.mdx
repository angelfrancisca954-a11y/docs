Business travel should be booked through the approved travel booking tool. This ensures duty-of-care coverage, centralized invoicing, and compliance with spend limits. Before making any booking, get your manager's approval — for trips over a certain threshold, director-level approval is also required (check with your manager).

<Warning>
  Trips booked outside the approved travel booking tool are **not covered by company travel insurance** and may not be fully reimbursed. Always book through the tool using your company email.
</Warning>

## Before You Book

Get your manager's approval before booking any trip. For trips over a certain threshold (check with your manager), director approval is also required. Once approved, book through the approved travel booking tool using your company email.

## Booking Guidelines

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Flights" icon="plane-departure">
    - Book economy class for flights under 5 hours.
    - Business class is permitted for flights over 8 hours, or over 6 hours if the trip spans multiple days of back-to-back meetings. Manager approval is required.
    - Book at least 14 days in advance when possible to reduce cost.
    - Non-refundable fares require manager approval before booking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hotels" icon="bed">
    - Use the preferred hotel list in the travel tool when available.
    - Rate caps vary by city — see the HR portal for current hotel rate caps, including rates for high-cost cities such as San Francisco, New York, and London.
    - Exceptions to the rate cap require manager approval **before** booking, not after.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ground Transport" icon="car">
    - Use rideshare or public transit for airport transfers whenever practical.
    - Rental cars are approved for multi-stop trips or locations without adequate transit options. Book mid-size or smaller vehicles.
    - Keep receipts for all ground transport and submit them with your expense report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## On the Road

Keep receipts for everything you spend while traveling. Submit all expenses within 30 days of returning. See [expense reimbursement](/policies/expense-reimbursement) for the full submission process.

Meals are covered on each travel day — see the HR portal for daily meal limits. Team dinners can exceed the standard limit with prior manager approval.

## Safety

Register your trip in the travel tool so the company always has a current record of your itinerary. If you're traveling to a country with an active travel advisory, contact HR before booking — additional steps may be required.

<Warning>
  In an emergency abroad: contact your travel insurer first (the card is available in the travel tool), then notify your manager and HR as soon as it's safe to do so.
</Warning>

## Cancellations

If a trip needs to be cancelled, do so as early as possible through the travel tool to minimize cancellation fees. Non-refundable bookings require manager approval before the booking is made — not after a cancellation becomes necessary.

<Note>
  Cancellation fees resulting from last-minute changes due to personal reasons may not be reimbursed. If a cancellation is due to a company decision, fees will be covered.
</Note>
